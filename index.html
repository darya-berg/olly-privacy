Olly Telegram Bot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Olly is a Telegram-first housing search assistant that helps users find rental listings in Israel. This Privacy Policy explains what data the bot receives, how it is used, and how users can request deletion.

Data We Collect

When you use Olly in Telegram, we may receive and store:

  • Telegram account basics such as user ID, chat ID, username, first name, and language.
  • Messages you send to the bot, including housing search requests and support requests.
  • Search preferences such as city, area, budget, number of rooms, size, and must-haves.
  • Bot interactions such as active searches, paused searches, listing feedback, and support messages.
  • Subscription or payment status if payments are enabled through Telegram.

We do not collect your Telegram password, payment card details, or private Telegram chats outside your direct interaction with the bot.

How We Use Data

We use this data to:

  • Understand your housing search request.
  • Send matching housing listings in Telegram.
  • Save and manage your active searches.
  • Provide support and respond to issues.
  • Improve matching quality, reliability, and abuse prevention.

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data and do not use it for advertising. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers used to run the bot, by Telegram as the messaging platform, and by payment providers if you make a payment through Telegram.

Limited bot data may be reviewed by the Olly team for support, debugging, safety, and product improvement.

Payments

If Telegram payments or Telegram Stars are used, payment processing is handled by Telegram and/or its payment providers. Olly does not store card numbers or banking details.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep user data only as long as needed to provide the bot service, maintain active searches, debug issues, and comply with operational or legal requirements.

To request deletion of your data, contact us through the bot using /help.

Telegram

Olly runs on Telegram. Telegram's own data practices are described in the Telegram Privacy Policy. If a separate policy is not specified for a bot, Telegram's Standard Privacy Policy for Bots and Mini Apps may apply.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us through @olly_home_bot using /help.
